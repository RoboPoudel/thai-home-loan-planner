Privacy policy – Home Loan Planner

Last updated: 26 November 2026

Home Loan Planner is a calculator and planning tool for home loans in Thailand. It is developed by Henry Poudel. It is not a bank or lender and does not offer, arrange or approve loans.

What the app stores

Everything you type – prices, income, payments, savings, notes, document photos and settings – is stored only on your own device, inside the app. We do not have servers that receive it, and we cannot see it.

What leaves your device

  • Bank rates and exchange rates. The app downloads a public list of bank interest rates from our GitHub Pages site and, if you use a foreign currency, public exchange rates from open.er-api.com. These requests send no personal data.
  • Files you choose to save or share. When you save a backup, PDF or picture, or share a summary, it goes only where you send it (for example your files, LINE or e-mail).
  • AI features (advisor, offer scan, contract check, voice) only work when the planner is opened inside Claude. They are not available in the Google Play app.

What we do not do

No account or sign-in. No advertising. No analytics or tracking. No selling or sharing of data. No access to your contacts, location or other apps.

Permissions (Android)

  • Notifications – only for payment and rate-change reminders you turn on.
  • Run at start-up – to restore those reminders after the phone restarts.
  • Internet – only to fetch the public rates above.

Deleting your data

Uninstall the app, or clear its storage in Android Settings → Apps → Home Loan Planner → Storage. Backup files you saved yourself stay where you saved them.

Children

The app is meant for adults planning a home purchase and is not directed at children.
