        Buying & owning

        THB

        Invest or prepay?

        Same monthly budget both ways

        Prepay puts the extra on the loan and, once it is paid off, invests the whole budget. Invest pays the normal installment and invests the extra from day one. Uses your rate plan, without the Plan tab's extra payments.

        What Thai banks ask for – tick as you collect

        Foreign buyers – key facts
        • Foreigners can own condos within the 49% foreign quota, not land.
        • Few banks lend to foreigners (UOB, ICBC; others usually need a work permit or a Thai spouse). Expect about 5–8% interest and 30–50% down.
        • Bring money in as foreign currency; get an FET form for transfers above USD 50,000.
        • Foreign buyers don't get the 0.01% fee cut – budget the normal 2% transfer fee.

              How to claim it

                Got extra money?

                A bonus, a gift or savings – pay down the loan, keep it safe or invest it?

                Paying down the loan "earns" your loan rate with no risk, minus any tax refund you lose. Deposit interest is taxed 15%. Investments can also lose money. Prepaying part of a loan usually has no fee; closing or refinancing within 3 years often costs about 3%.
